IllusiaCase study · 13

Joby Aviation · Texas · Project Nexus

Every partner, official and press contact is a channel.

A nine-day aviation milestone turned into a communications engine that travelled far beyond the event itself.

The moment

Joby's Texas eIPP / Project Nexus activation brought flight operations, government officials, aviation leaders, strategic partners and media around a major step toward commercial electric aviation in the U.S.

What we changed

We embedded with the team for the whole trip, so the story was never one announcement and a handful of event photos. We captured the voices behind the milestone — officials and partners explaining why the program mattered — alongside the aircraft, demonstrations, people and operational context the story needed to read visually.

How the story travelled

Every night we turned that day's coverage into usable assets: photo selects, recap videos and media-ready string-outs that could move immediately across Joby's channels, partner accounts, stakeholder comms, PR teams and press — while the news cycle was still live.

From documentation to distribution

The value was not better footage. It was giving Joby and the people around the program the right assets, fast enough to keep amplifying the story as it unfolded.

Owned social Partner & executive channels Government & stakeholder channels PR & press Industry media

Capture → Rapid delivery → Stakeholder amplification → Earned media. One milestone, many narratives, more places for the story to be picked up.
