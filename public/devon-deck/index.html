The idea · 04

The idea · Sweet Honey Farm × the drop

A launch campaign for the farm and merch drop.

The farm is the world and the merch is the thread through it. A golden-hour hero film follows a runner in the Horsepower Ascents and a horse at full stride through a day on the farm (the garden, the animals, the cold plunge, the community) and ends with the two side by side. Around it, the story of the drop: blueprints on the table, pieces styled into the land, worn by the people who live there. One shoot day that launches the collection, stands on its own, and cuts down for social.
