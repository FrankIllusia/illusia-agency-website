Your version of it

For St. Rose it is the water four blocks from the building.

You have your own. The thirty-plus middle schools that feed you. An athletics programme families change towns for. A campus most applicants have never walked. None of that is a beach, and none of it needs to be.

The work is finding the one thing a family cannot get anywhere else, and then shooting it like it matters.
