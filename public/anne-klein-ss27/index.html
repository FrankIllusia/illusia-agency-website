CAPTURE PRIORITIES 02
ANNE KLEIN SPRING SUMMER 2027

Second unit capture priorities.

01 · HIGHEST PRIORITY

Product-first vignettes & stills

Target 3–4 distinct product-focused vignettes for each category, with corresponding still photography. Capture macro details, material/texture, hardware, styling gestures, product-in-motion and artful compositions. Each vignette should generate several clean clips and alternate angles.

02

Jasmine Tookes: social-first moments & B-roll

Candid on-set footage, glam and styling (TBD), transitions between looks, walking and natural movement, outfit reveals, close-ups and interactions with key accessories. Where schedule and approvals permit, a handful of low-lift, intentional social concepts. Avoid interrupting hero-unit direction.

03

Campaign extensions & evergreen library

Environmental details, editorial motion, set atmosphere, transitional shots, clean cutaways and flexible content for launch, monthly drops, licensee spotlights, Stories, Reels and future edits.

VIGNETTE DIRECTION 03
SHORT-FORM VIGNETTE CREATIVE DIRECTION

Four vignettes per category, 5–15 seconds each.

1 OF 4

Standard, elevated brand / product showcase

A more traditional, analog-style execution highlighting the product, its details and styling in a polished, premium way.

3 OF 4

Creative, thumb-stopping social content

Where we differentiate: unexpected angles, dynamic movement, interesting transitions, playful product interactions and more engaging storytelling.

PRODUCTION STYLE

A mix of cinematic, highly produced content and elevated, high-quality UGC-style footage captured by Jordin. Variety in both creative treatment and production style, while keeping a cohesive Anne Klein aesthetic.

ON SET

All concepts achievable within the existing campaign set and production environment, maximizing the content captured without disrupting the primary shoot.
